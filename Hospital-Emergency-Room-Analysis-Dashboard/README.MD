# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard

## 📘 Overview
The **Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to monitor, analyze, and optimize emergency room (ER) operations.  
It provides a comprehensive view of patient flow, wait times, admissions, referrals, and demographic trends, enabling healthcare administrators and decision-makers to improve efficiency and patient experience.

This dashboard supports **monthly analysis**, **long-term consolidated insights**, and **patient-level drill-downs** through an intuitive, user-friendly interface.

## 🎯 Objectives
- To monitor ER patient volume and visit patterns over time.  
- To analyze average wait times and service-level performance.  
- To track admission rates and departmental referrals.  
- To understand patient demographics (age, gender, race).  
- To identify peak days and hours for better staff and resource planning.  
- To support data-driven operational and clinical decisions.

---

## 🔑 Key Features
- **KPI Snapshot:** Instant view of total patients, average wait time, satisfaction score, and referrals.  
- **Time-Based Analysis:** Monthly and consolidated views for trend identification.  
- **Service Level Monitoring:** Percentage of patients seen within 30 minutes.  
- **Demographic Insights:** Age group, gender, and race-based patient distribution.  
- **Operational Analysis:** Day-wise and hour-wise patient traffic heatmap.  
- **Referral Tracking:** Department-wise referral patterns.  
- **Patient Drill-Down:** Detailed, record-level patient information for deeper analysis.  
- **Interactive Filters:** Date slicers, month-year selection, and bookmarks for seamless navigation.

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPIs, calculated measures, and performance metrics  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and modeling

## 🗂️ Dashboard Structure

This Power BI report consists of **multiple views**, navigated using bookmarks:

---

### 📅 Monthly View
Provides a focused snapshot of ER performance for a selected month.

**Key Insights Include:**
1️⃣ Total number of patients  
2️⃣ Average wait time (in minutes)  
3️⃣ Patient satisfaction score  
4️⃣ Number of patients referred  
5️⃣ Admission vs. non-admission split  
6️⃣ % of patients seen within 30 minutes  
7️⃣ Patient distribution by age group  
8️⃣ Patient distribution by gender  
9️⃣ Department referral breakdown  
🔟 Day & hour-wise patient volume heatmap  

---

### 📊 Consolidated View
Offers a long-term, aggregated analysis across a selected date range.

**Highlights:**
1️⃣ Overall ER patient volume  
2️⃣ Long-term wait time trends  
3️⃣ Average patient satisfaction score  
4️⃣ Total referrals across departments  
5️⃣ Admission trends over time  
6️⃣ Demographic analysis (age, gender, race)  
7️⃣ Peak day and hour identification for capacity planning  

---

### 👤 Patient Details View
Enables granular, row-level analysis of individual ER visits.

**Includes:**
- Patient ID & Name  
- Gender & Age  
- Admission date  
- Patient race  
- Wait time  
- Department referral  
- Admission status  

This view supports detailed audits, investigations, and operational deep dives.

---

### 📌 Key Takeaways View
Summarizes high-level insights and actionable observations derived from the data to support executive decision-making.
