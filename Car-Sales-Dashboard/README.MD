# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard

## 📘 Overview
The **Car Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI solution designed to analyze and monitor automobile sales performance across multiple dimensions such as time, region, company, body style, color, and dealer networks.

This dashboard provides **year-to-date (YTD)** and **month-to-date (MTD)** insights, sales trends, geographic performance, and transaction-level drill-downs, enabling business users, analysts, and stakeholders to make **data-driven sales and pricing decisions**.

The report is structured with **high-level executive KPIs**, **visual trend analysis**, and **detailed transactional views**, all supported by **dynamic filters** for deep exploration.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Track overall car sales revenue and growth performance  
- Analyze average selling price trends over time  
- Monitor total cars sold with YTD and MTD comparisons  
- Understand sales distribution by body style, color, and brand  
- Identify top-performing dealer regions geographically  
- Enable record-level analysis of individual car sales transactions  
- Support strategic decisions in pricing, inventory, and regional expansion  

---

## 🔑 Key Features
- **Executive KPI Cards**: YTD Total Sales, YTD Average Price, and YTD Cars Sold with growth indicators  
- **MTD Comparison Metrics**: Month-to-date sales, average price, and units sold  
- **Weekly Sales Trend**: Visual representation of YTD weekly sales performance  
- **Category Analysis**: Sales breakdown by body style and car color  
- **Geographic Insights**: Dealer region-wise car sales mapped visually  
- **Company Performance**: Brand-wise comparison of sales, units sold, and growth contribution  
- **Detailed Sales Table**: Transaction-level details with customer, dealer, car, and pricing information  
- **Interactive Filters**: Body style, dealer name, transmission type, and engine type slicers  
- **Multi-Page Navigation**: Dashboard and detailed views using bookmarks

## 🗂️ Dashboard Structure

### 📊 Dashboard View (Sales Overview)
Provides a high-level snapshot of sales performance.  

**Key Insights Include:**
1. YTD Total Sales with growth percentage  
2. YTD Average Car Price with price variance  
3. YTD Cars Sold with growth trend  
4. Weekly sales trend visualization  
5. Sales distribution by body style (SUV, Sedan, Hatchback, etc.)  
6. Sales distribution by car color  
7. Dealer region-wise car sales (map view)  
8. Company-wise sales, units sold, and growth contribution  

This view is designed for **executives and decision-makers** who need fast, actionable insights.  

---

### 📋 Details View (Transactional Analysis)
Enables deep, record-level exploration of individual car sales.  

**Includes:**
- Car ID  
- Sale Date  
- Customer Name  
- Dealer Name  
- Company (Brand)  
- Car Color  
- Car Model  
- Total Sales Amount  

This view supports **audits, sales investigations, dealer performance analysis, and customer-level insights**.  

---

## 📌 Filters & Interactivity
The dashboard includes dynamic slicers for:  
- Body Style  
- Dealer Name  
- Transmission Type  
- Engine Type  
- Date Range  

These filters update all visuals in real time, enabling **seamless drill-down and focused analysis**.
