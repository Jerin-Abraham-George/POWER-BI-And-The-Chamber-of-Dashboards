# 🚗 Car Sales Dashboard – Problem Statement

## 📘 Background
Our company operates as a car dealership offering a wide range of automobile models. To effectively track and analyze sales performance, we require a comprehensive **Car Sales Dashboard** built in **Power BI**. This dashboard will serve as a centralized solution to monitor key metrics, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objective
The primary objective of this project is to **design and develop a dynamic, interactive Car Sales Dashboard** using Power BI. The dashboard will visualize critical KPIs related to car sales, enabling stakeholders to evaluate performance over time and make informed business decisions.

---

## 📊 Problem Statement 1: KPI Requirements
The dashboard must provide **real-time insights** into key performance indicators (KPIs) to support strategic monitoring and growth analysis.  

### 1. Sales Overview
- Year-to-Date (YTD) Total Sales  
- Month-to-Date (MTD) Total Sales  
- Year-over-Year (YOY) Growth in Total Sales  
- Difference between YTD Sales and Previous Year-to-Date (PTYD) Sales  

### 2. Average Price Analysis
- YTD Average Price  
- MTD Average Price  
- YOY Growth in Average Price  
- Difference between YTD Average Price and PTYD Average Price  

### 3. Cars Sold Metrics
- YTD Cars Sold  
- MTD Cars Sold  
- YOY Growth in Cars Sold  
- Difference between YTD Cars Sold and PTYD Cars Sold
