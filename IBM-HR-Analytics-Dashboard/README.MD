# 📊 IBM HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI HR Analytics dashboard** designed to provide deep insights into employee attrition, career growth, compensation fairness, performance, and satisfaction—helping HR leaders make data-driven workforce decisions.

---

## 🔍 Overview
The IBM HR Analytics Dashboard consolidates key workforce metrics into a single, intuitive Power BI solution.  
It enables organizations to identify attrition risks, promotion delays, compensation inequities, and satisfaction gaps across:

- Departments  
- Job roles  
- Education levels  
- Age groups  
- Gender  

This dashboard is ideal for **HR analytics, people strategy, and leadership decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- 📉 Analyze employee attrition patterns and early exits  
- 🚀 Evaluate promotion delays and career stagnation risks  
- 💰 Assess salary growth, performance pay, and compensation fairness  
- 😊 Measure job satisfaction and work-life balance  
- 🔎 Identify high-risk job roles and departments for proactive HR action  

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🔁 Attrition & Workforce Metrics
- Overall attrition rate, active employees, and early attrition (within 1 year)  
- Attrition % and turnover cost by department  
- Tenure-adjusted attrition analysis  
- Attrition drivers segmented by department, age group, and gender  
- Education-wise attrition causes (promotion/salary vs. natural progression)  

### 🚀 Promotion & Career Growth Metrics
- Promotion Gap Indicator (early vs. delayed promotions)  
- Promotion gap by job role and department  
- Average promotion delay (in years) by role  
- Stagnation Risk Analysis (High / Medium / Low)  
- Identification of roles vulnerable to career stagnation and disengagement  

### 💰 Performance & Salary Metrics
- Average salary hike %  
- Compensation Fairness Index  
- Performance Pay Ratio analysis  
- Salary growth by job role and education level  
- Performance vs. pay alignment across age buckets  
- Identification of compensation inequity hotspots  

### 😊 Satisfaction Metrics
- Overall Job Satisfaction Score  
- Work-Life Balance score by department and age group  
- Satisfaction distribution (Low → Very High) by job role  
- Performance vs. satisfaction comparison using bubble analysis  
- Clear indicators for underperforming and at-risk roles  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard design & visualization  
- **DAX** – KPIs, calculated measures, and indexes  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation  
- **Excel / CSV** – HR analytics sample dataset  

---

## 🧱 Dashboard Structure
1️⃣ **Attrition & Workforce Overview**  
- Attrition rate, early exits, turnover cost  
- Department-level and demographic analysis  
- Attrition drivers and education impact  

2️⃣ **Promotion & Career Growth Analysis**  
- Promotion gap KPIs  
- Role-wise and department-wise promotion delays  
- Career stagnation risk assessment  

3️⃣ **Performance & Salary Analysis**  
- Salary hikes and performance pay ratios  
- Compensation fairness index  
- Education and age-based salary trends  

4️⃣ **Satisfaction & Engagement Analysis**  
- Overall satisfaction score  
- Work-life balance insights  
- Role-wise satisfaction vs. performance mapping  

---

## ✅ Key Insights & Outcomes
- 📌 Early attrition is highest in specific departments and age groups  
- 📌 Several roles show promotion delays leading to stagnation risk  
- 📌 Compensation inequity exists across job roles despite similar performance  
- 📌 Satisfaction and performance do not always move together  
- 📌 Actionable insights for retention, promotion planning, and pay correction
