# 🍽️ Zomato Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
The Zomato Sales Performance Dashboard is an interactive **Power BI** solution designed to analyze and visualize food delivery business performance across multiple cities. It provides a comprehensive view of sales, orders, user activity, and customer engagement, enabling data-driven insights into operational and market trends.

This dashboard helps stakeholders monitor city-level performance, understand customer behavior, and identify growth opportunities across regions and time periods.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Analyze sales performance across different cities  
- Track order volume and customer engagement trends  
- Monitor user growth and churn (gained vs. lost users)  
- Identify top-performing locations and categories  
- Understand demographic patterns such as age and gender distribution  
- Enable strategic decision-making through interactive visual insights  

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🌍 City Performance Analysis
- Sales, orders, gained users, and lost users by city  
- Identification of top-performing and underperforming locations  
- Dynamic filtering by city, year, and date range  

### 📊 Sales & Order Insights
- Total sales amount (~987M) and order volume (~301K)  
- City-wise sales comparison with ranking  
- Category-level performance (Veg, Non-Veg, Others)  

### 📈 Trend Analysis
- Year-wise sales trend visualization  
- Growth patterns and seasonal insights  
- Historical performance tracking from 2017 to 2020  

### 👥 User Behavior Analytics
- Active users and total user count  
- Gained vs. lost users with gender distribution  
- Insights into customer retention and churn  

### 🎯 Demographic Insights
- Users segmented by age groups  
- Identification of dominant customer segments  
- Behavioral patterns across different age brackets  

### ⭐ Ratings & Engagement
- Total ratings (~148K) across cities  
- Rating distribution by city  
- Correlation between ratings and sales performance  

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard design, visualization, and data modeling  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Custom KPIs and calculated measures  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation  
- **Excel/CSV Files** – Data source and preprocessing  

---

## 🧭 Dashboard Structure

### 1️⃣ Overview Page
- High-level KPIs: Sales, Quantity, Ratings, Orders  
- Category-wise sales breakdown (Veg, Non-Veg, Others)  
- Top cities by sales  
- Year-wise sales trend  

### 2️⃣ City Performance Page
- Detailed comparison of cities based on:
  - Sales  
  - Orders  
  - User growth (gain vs. loss)  
- Ranking of cities by performance  
- Rating and user distribution across cities  

### 3️⃣ User Performance Page
- Active users and total user base  
- Gained vs. lost users (gender-based analysis)  
- User distribution by age group  
- Insights into customer engagement patterns  

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- Tirupati and Electronic City (Bangalore) are among the top-performing cities in terms of sales  
- Significant user churn observed despite steady user acquisition  
- Peak sales observed around 2018, followed by a gradual decline  
- Majority of users fall within the 20–26 age group  
- Veg category slightly outperforms Non-Veg in total sales
