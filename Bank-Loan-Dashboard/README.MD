# 🏦 Bank Loan Dashboard – Loan Performance & Risk Insights

## 📌 Overview
The **Bank Loan Dashboard** is a comprehensive Power BI analytics solution designed to monitor loan performance, borrower behavior, and portfolio risk across multiple dimensions.  
It provides a **360° view** of loan applications, funded amounts, repayments, interest rates, and credit risk segmentation.

This dashboard enables financial analysts, risk teams, and decision-makers to evaluate loan quality, track growth trends, and identify potential risk areas with clarity and speed.
