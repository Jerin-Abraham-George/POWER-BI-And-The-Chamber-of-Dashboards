# 📑 Bank Loan Report – Domain Knowledge

## 📌 Introduction
Bank loans are a fundamental financial instrument that empower individuals and businesses to achieve their objectives and manage financial requirements effectively.  
To make informed borrowing decisions, it is essential for applicants to understand loan terms, associated costs, and repayment responsibilities.

Banks collect and manage loan-related data through multiple channels and processes, which serve as the foundation for risk assessment, portfolio management, and regulatory compliance.
